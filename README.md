SC Helper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SC Helper is a browser extension for office staff. It runs entirely inside your browser.

What SC Helper handles: When you use the tools, SC Helper reads information shown on the web page you have open, such as student names, the last 4 digits of a Social Security number, payment plan amounts, dates, contact details, ISIR answers and federal loan balances. It does this only after you click the extension button, and it only reads: it never clicks, submits or changes anything on the page.

Where it goes: Information read from pages is kept in your browser's session memory only, so you can fill in and download your documents and compare pages. It is erased when you close the browser, or sooner if you press "Erase now" in Settings. It is never written to disk, never sent to the developer, and never sent to any third party. SC Helper makes no network requests and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What is saved on your computer: Your own settings: school name, the blank form file you choose to load, light or dark mode, your pay-date calendar, which version notes and beta notices you have seen, and your Advanced notes setting.

Your notes: the Note Generator saves the choices and text you enter in your notes in your browser profile on your computer, so they are still there after you close the browser. If you turn on Advanced notes, you can keep several notes and name them; a new note's name may start as the student name shown by another tool, and you can change it. Notes are never sent anywhere. You can erase all saved notes at any time with "Erase notes" in Settings, and removing the extension deletes them. If you export a note, it is saved as a file where you choose.

What we do not do: We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide credit or lending. We do not collect it at all, because it never leaves your device.

Permissions: activeTab and scripting let the extension read the page you are viewing when you ask it to. storage keeps your settings, your notes and session memory. A content script on cod.ed.gov only removes dashes from a Social Security number you paste into a field there.

Independence: SC Helper is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any school, software vendor or government agency, including the U.S. Department of Education.
