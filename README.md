SC Helper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SC Helper is a browser extension for office staff. It runs entirely inside your browser.

What SC Helper handles: When you use the tools, SC Helper reads information shown on the web page you have open, such as student names, Social Security numbers, birth dates, payment plan amounts, dates, contact details, ISIR answers and federal loan balances. It does this only after you click the extension button or start a tool.

Most tools only read the page. NSLDS Printout, which you start yourself, also moves through NSLDS pages and fills the student's Social Security number into NSLDS's own forms so it can save those pages as a PDF. It never submits anything to anyone other than the NSLDS site you are already using.

Where it goes: Information read from pages is kept in your browser's session memory only, so you can fill in and download your documents and compare pages. It is erased when you close the browser, or sooner if you press "Erase now" in Settings. It is never written to disk by SC Helper, never sent to the developer, and never sent to any third party. SC Helper makes no network requests and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Files you create are saved where you choose. NSLDS Printout and the APF Generator save PDFs through your browser's Save As window. The optional click-to-copy feature and the Prior Schools copy put text on your computer's clipboard when you click; like anything you copy, it stays there until you copy something else.

What is saved on your computer: Only your own settings: school name, the blank form file you choose to load, light or dark mode, 12-hour or 24-hour time, sound effects, your pay-date calendar, which tool you used last, which version notes and beta notices you have seen, and which optional features you turned on (the COD Social Security paste fix and the Education Portal helpers). No student information is saved.

What we do not do: We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide credit or lending. We do not collect it at all, because it never leaves your device.

Permissions: activeTab and scripting let the extension read the page you are viewing when you ask it to. storage keeps your settings and session memory. debugger is used only by NSLDS Printout, to save the student's NSLDS pages as a PDF. downloads (optional, asked for when you save) is used only to show the Save As window. Access to the Education Portal (optional) is asked for only if you turn on one of the Education Portal helpers in Settings; it lets the extension show click-to-copy, estimated quarters and the student's age on the EP General tab. A content script on cod.ed.gov only removes dashes from a Social Security number you paste into a field there.

Independence: SC Helper is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any school, software vendor or government agency, including the U.S. Department of Education.
